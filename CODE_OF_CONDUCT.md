# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
Assume the other party is competent and acting in good faith, because they
usually are.

Specifically:

- Critique the code, the design or the decision. Never the author.
- Explain your reasoning. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this breaks tenant
  isolation because the query bypasses the scoped client" does.
- Accept a well-argued disagreement gracefully. Being persuaded is not losing.
- Ask when you do not know. Nobody here has read all 46 documents.

Not acceptable: personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language,
publishing anyone's private information, or sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Raise it with the project maintainer at hefnyamr@rocketmail.com. Reports are
handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private word to removal from
the project, proportionate to what happened.
